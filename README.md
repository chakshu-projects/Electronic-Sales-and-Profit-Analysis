# Electronic-Sales-and-Profit-Analysis

## Overview  
     This project provides an analysis of Electronic store data from 2020 to 2022, focusing to increase their profit and    
     sales.The data was analyzed and transformed in Power BI to create interactive dashboard and meaningful business insights.  
     Through visualizations the project provides a clear overview of business growth and customer purchasing behaviour.

## Objectives  
   - Analyze the overall sales and profit performance
   - Identify the top-performing products and category
   - Understanding customer purchasing trends
   - Supporting business decision-making with data-driven insights
   - Compare the sales of the different years
## Dataset Description  
   - Product details(Usage, Product name, Price, Segment)  
   - Sales data(Revenue, Quantity, Profit)  
   - Customer details(Customer name, Customer ID)  
   - Regional data  
   - Order information(Order_ID, Order_date)
## Tools & Technologies Used  
   - Power BI  
   - Data Cleaning & Transforming
   - Data Modelling
   - DAX(Data Analysis Expression)
   - Data Visualizations
## Dashboard Pages     
  ### 1) Sales Analysis    
     Foncused on:   
  - Product-wise Sales  
  - Category Performance
  - Weekly Trends of Sales
  - Compare Sales Analysis

  ### 2) Profit Analysis  
       Includes the:
   - Monthly Trends of Profit
   - Category-wise Profit Comparison
   - High and Low Profit Regions
   - Business Profitability Insights
     
## Key Insights  
   - The month July has highest Sales and Profit
   - West Region contributed highest Revenue
   - SSD 256GB is the most Profitable Product
   - The year 2020 has the Highest Sales and Profit
